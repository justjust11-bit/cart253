## Entry 5: October 16th 2026

I finished my third Events prototype today. I spent a lot of time making my prototypes since it is Reading Week, so I knew I would have more time. I made three prototypes that have different user interactions, for different types of experiences. I think it's cool when the user can directly see how their interaction shows up on the program, so in all of them I added some kind of output that they can directly see the result of their personal actions; the clicking counter in _Taiko Rush!!_, the stamps with the clicking and dragging effect in _Make Your Own Scene_, the text that displays the tapping accuracy in _One Note Rythm Game_~~these: PERFECT, GREAT, GOOD, BAD, MISS~~, as well as the general score that builds up. My prototypes all user interaction on a smaller scale. I admit for _One Note Rythm Game_, the song has a lot of instrumentals, so it's a little difficult to hear the beat, but a big part what makes me enjoy rythm games is having good music, so I chose a song I love. This prototype was the most difficult to make out of all them, because to accurately identify time spent and bpm, and to make it work in code required math, and I am not very good at math, so I got help from someone who is to help me figure out a way to make the game work. It made me realize just how even simple programming can be benefited and simplified by using math. It helps that if I have to learn math, it will be while working on projects I make myself that I am interested in, and are practical.
